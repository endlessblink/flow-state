## Design Context

### Users
FlowState serves people organizing real work who need to choose the next task, sustain focus, and move through a backlog without cognitive overload.

### Brand Personality
Calm, grounded, and purposeful. The interface should feel warm and focused rather than gamified, futuristic, or visually loud.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use FlowState's warm-charcoal surfaces, warm off-white text, teal-green signature accent, Clash Display headings, compact task metadata, flat panels, and restrained elevation. Support dark and light themes, RTL, reduced motion, and keyboard use. Avoid purple AI gradients, decorative glow, excessive glass, and generic dashboard card grids.

### Design Principles
- Put the current action first and progressively disclose the backlog.
- Use the teal accent only for active state and meaningful interaction.
- Preserve warm neutral surfaces and readable information hierarchy.
- Make task movement, direction, and state understandable without decoration.
- Keep every interaction accessible in LTR, RTL, mouse, touch, and keyboard contexts.
